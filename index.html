What we build

Three ways we take work off your team's plate.

Every engagement starts with mapping how work actually moves through your organization today — then we replace the slow parts with a system that runs itself.

01

Process automation

We rebuild repetitive, rule-based work — intake forms, approvals, reconciliations — as automated pipelines with full audit trails.

02

AI agents & orchestration

For judgment-heavy steps, we deploy agents that read documents, make decisions within guardrails you set, and escalate what's genuinely ambiguous.

03

Systems integration

We connect the tools you already use — CRM, ERP, ticketing, spreadsheets — so information moves between them without anyone copying and pasting.

Engagement

From audit to live pipeline in four steps.

This is a real sequence — each step depends on what came before it, and we don't skip ahead.

STEP 1

Map the current process

We shadow the team doing the work and document every handoff, exception, and workaround — not just the process on paper.

STEP 2

Design the workflow

We propose exactly which steps get automated, which stay human, and where an agent should ask for approval rather than act alone.

STEP 3

Build and test in parallel

The new workflow runs alongside the old one until its output matches or beats it — nothing goes live on faith.

STEP 4

Hand off and monitor

Your team gets a dashboard showing what's running, what's flagged for review, and where time is actually being saved.
