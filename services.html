Services

Pick where your team is losing the most time — we'll start there.

Most engagements begin narrow: one workflow, fully automated and proven, before we expand. Below are the three areas we work in most.

01 · Process automation

Replace repetitive, rule-based work with a pipeline.

If the process follows the same steps every time — intake, data entry, reconciliation, status updates — it's a strong candidate to run without a person doing it manually.

Document intake

Forms, PDFs, and emails are read, validated, and entered into your systems automatically, with exceptions routed to a human.

Approvals & routing

Requests move to the right approver based on rules you set, with reminders and escalation built in — no more chasing sign-off over email.

Reconciliation

Line-by-line matching across spreadsheets, invoices, and statements, with discrepancies flagged instead of buried.

02 · AI agents & orchestration

For the steps that need judgment, not just rules.

Agents read context, apply the guardrails you define, and act — or ask. You decide what they're trusted to decide alone.

Document understanding

Agents extract meaning from contracts, claims, and correspondence — not just fields, but intent — and classify what needs attention.

Decision support

An agent proposes the next action with its reasoning attached, so your team reviews a recommendation instead of starting from a blank page.

Escalation logic

You set the thresholds for what an agent can resolve on its own versus what always goes to a person — and can tighten or loosen them anytime.

03 · Systems integration

Make your existing tools talk to each other.

We rarely replace what you already run. Instead, we connect it — so information entered once shows up everywhere it's needed.

CRM & ERP sync

Customer and order data stays consistent across systems without anyone re-keying it by hand.

Ticketing & support tools

Cases open, update, and close automatically as work moves through your pipeline — your support tool reflects reality in real time.

Spreadsheet retirement

Where a spreadsheet has quietly become mission-critical infrastructure, we replace it with a system that has version control and access rules.
