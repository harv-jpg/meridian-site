About Meridian

We got tired of watching good teams do bad work — not because they were careless, but because the process forced it.

Meridian was founded by people who spent years inside operations teams before building tools for them. We build workflows the way we wish someone had built ours.

Our approach

Automation should remove drudgery, not oversight.

We don't believe in automating a process just because it's technically possible. Every workflow we build keeps a clear line of sight for the people responsible for the outcome — what ran, what was flagged, and why.

01

Start with the real process

Not the flowchart in the handbook — what people actually do, including the workarounds that keep things moving today.

02

Prove it before it's trusted

Every pipeline runs in parallel with the old process until its output holds up, exception by exception.

03

Keep a human in reach

Agents escalate what's genuinely ambiguous instead of guessing — your team sets where that line sits.

04

Leave you able to run it

You get a system your own team can read, adjust, and hand off — not a black box only we can maintain.
