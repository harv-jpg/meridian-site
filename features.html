Features

A CRM backbone, with an AI layer that does the parts you hate.

Everything below is either the core pipeline every CRM needs, or the AI automation that makes Meridian different from Salesforce, HubSpot, or a spreadsheet.

01 · Pipeline & contacts

The backbone — light enough to set up in ten minutes.

No admin, no mandatory fields, no onboarding call. Add a contact and start working.

Simple kanban pipeline

Lead → Proposal Sent → Negotiating → Won/Lost. Drag a deal forward as it moves — that's the whole model.

Contact timeline

Every email, call note, and document tied to a client lives on one page, in order, so you never dig for context before a call.

Ten-minute setup

Connect your inbox and you're working. No required fields, no admin console, no seat management for a team of one.

02 · AI automation — the differentiator

Six things Meridian does without being asked.

This is what actually saves time: not a chat window, but the tedious work happening in the background.

Auto-logging

Connects to email and calendar, detects new leads, and logs conversations automatically — no manual data entry, ever.

Draft assistant

Drafts follow-ups, proposals, and check-ins based on context. "It's been 2 weeks since the quote" becomes a ready-to-send nudge.

Meeting-to-notes

Transcribes Zoom and Meet calls, then auto-fills a summary and next steps straight into the client record.

Proposal generator

Turns a scoped conversation into a formatted proposal or contract draft, styled after your own past proposals.

Deal-risk flags

Flags stalling deals by value and silence — "$4k deal, no reply in 10 days — want a nudge drafted?"

Smart invoicing reminders

Drafts polite or firm payment reminders based on how overdue the invoice is and how that client has responded before.

03 · Built for freelancers

Past the handshake, still in one place.

These usually live in three other tools. Here, they're attached to the same client record as everything else.

01

Contracts + e-signature

Built into the client record, not bolted on as a separate app.

02

Time tracking

Tied directly to a client and project from the moment you start the clock.

03

One-click invoicing

Generate an invoice from tracked time or a fixed-fee milestone in a click.

04

Revenue dashboard

Pipeline value, monthly recurring vs. one-off income, and win rate in one view.

See it running on your own inbox.
