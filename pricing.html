Questions

A few things people ask before signing up.

Q

What counts as an "active client"?

Anyone with an open deal in your pipeline or an ongoing project. Past or closed clients don't count toward the Starter limit.

Q

Can agencies of 2–5 people use this?

Yes — Setu works well for small teams sharing a pipeline. Team seats beyond one user are part of the Pro plan.

Q

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No. Pro is billed monthly and you can cancel anytime — your data stays exportable if you leave.
